Limits and privacy

What the numbers mean

Scores are simple averages from 1 to 5. They show what each person reported. A difference means the same relationship may feel different from each side. It does not tell you who is right.

How patterns are chosen

The tool uses simple display rules to decide which patterns to show first. These rules are not clinical cut-offs. They are only a way to make the map easier to read.

How Jungian ideas are used

Jungian ideas such as shadow, projection, complexes, anima/animus, and individuation are used as reflection lenses. The tool does not claim to measure the unconscious.

Privacy

The page does not use analytics, cookies, external JavaScript, or a questionnaire database. Couple Codes can contain raw structured answers, so treat them as personal. The deeper-pattern topic is excluded unless you choose to include it. Quick hide does not erase browser history.

Selected foundations

Jungian: C. G. Jung, Psychological Types, Aion, The Psychology of the Transference, and Marriage as a Psychological Relationship, alongside contemporary Jungian couple work.

Couple work: attachment theory, Emotionally Focused Couple Therapy, Integrative Behavioral Couple Therapy, demand-withdraw research, mentalization, repair, responsiveness, and relationship-process research.

The questions and scoring in this tool are original. They do not reproduce a validated clinical instrument.

Safety checklist

Do not use this as a shared couple exercise if you cannot answer freely and safely. The tool assumes both people can disagree, say no, set boundaries, and speak honestly without fear of punishment.

It is better not to use this tool together if any of these apply:

  • You are afraid of how your partner may react to your answers, boundaries, or disagreement.
  • Your partner controls or closely monitors your movements, money, work, social contacts, phone, messages, accounts, or location.
  • There have been threats, intimidation, stalking, property damage, harm to pets, restraint, choking, physical violence, or threats involving weapons.
  • There has been sexual pressure, sexual activity without free consent, or punishment after you say no.
  • You fear retaliation if you seek help, tell someone what is happening, or try to leave.
  • You are being pressured to complete this tool, show your answers, or share a code.
  • You suspect this device or account is being monitored and seeing this page could put you at risk.
  • You cannot speak honestly because your answers may be used against you later.
In these situations, the problem is not ordinary miscommunication. A joint relationship exercise can increase risk or give a controlling partner more information. Consider private support from a qualified professional or specialist service instead. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

If you use parts of the tool privately, consider a safer device. Quick hide does not erase browser history or other digital traces.
