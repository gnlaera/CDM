Interpretive limits and research notes

Why this is formulation, not diagnosis

Relationship distress is not a single disease entity. Different therapy schools organize the same interaction differently. This tool therefore uses converging lenses and looks for interaction hypotheses, protective factors, and leverage points. No score proves incompatibility or predicts whether a relationship will last.

Jungian anchor

The analytical frame draws on Jung's Psychological Types, Aion, The Psychology of the Transference, and Marriage as a Psychological Relationship. The key practical question is where an ego-to-ego conversation shifts into projection, shadow defense, complex activation, or an archetypally amplified enactment.

Evidence-based couple therapy

Modern outcome research supports couple therapy overall, including behavioral and emotionally focused approaches. Integrative Behavioral Couple Therapy adds acceptance to behavior change. Demand-withdraw is a well-studied destructive cycle. Mentalization-based couple work focuses on recovering curiosity about two separate minds. Evidence remains heterogeneous, and no single framework should be treated as a universal diagnostic truth.

Safety

Fear, coercive control, violence, sexual coercion, stalking, or retaliation require a safety-first response. Joint exercises can be unsafe when one person cannot speak freely. This tool does not assess lethality and is not a substitute for confidential professional risk assessment.
